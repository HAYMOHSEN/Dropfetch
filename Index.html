1. Who we are

Dropfetch is published by Jotech (Amman, Jordan). Contact: haymohsen@gmail.com. This policy explains what the Dropfetch desktop app for Windows does with information, and – mostly – what it does not do.

2. Information we collect

None. Dropfetch has no accounts, no telemetry, no crash reporting service and no analytics. Jotech does not receive the links you paste, the files you convert, your settings or your download history.

3. What stays on your PC

  • Settings (download folder, quality, options) – stored in your Windows user profile.
  • Library – a local list of files you saved or converted, so you can find them again. You can clear it at any time from the Library view.
  • Diagnostic log – a small text file recording job events and errors to help you troubleshoot. It never leaves your computer unless you choose to copy it when asking for support.

Uninstalling Dropfetch removes the app; downloaded and converted files remain yours in the folders you chose.

4. Network connections the app makes

  • The site you paste a link to. To save a video, Dropfetch's download engine connects directly from your PC to that website, exactly like a browser would. That website sees your IP address and the usual request details, under its own privacy policy.
  • Thumbnails. The preview image shown in the app is loaded from the same site.
  • Engine updates. Once a day, and when you press "Update engine", the app contacts GitHub to check for and download the latest version of the open-source download engine (yt-dlp). GitHub sees a standard download request from your IP address.

No request is ever sent to Jotech servers. The app works fully offline for conversions.

5. Optional features you control

  • Browser cookies. If you choose a browser under Settings → Borrow sign-in cookies, the engine reads that browser's cookie store on your PC so sites that require sign-in can be accessed. Cookies are used only for the request to that site and are never stored or transmitted by Dropfetch. This is off by default.
  • Clipboard watching. If enabled, Dropfetch checks your clipboard locally for video links and offers to save them. Clipboard content is never stored or sent anywhere. Off by default.
  • Proxy. If you enter a proxy address, downloads are routed through it; the proxy operator may see your traffic.

6. Children

Dropfetch is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

7. Your responsibilities

Please only download content you own, that is licensed for download, or that you have permission to save, and respect each website's terms of service and the rights of creators.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.
